## Tony Elumelu Foundation Entrepreneurship
- **Provider:** Tony Elumelu Foundation
- **URL:** [www.TEFConnect.com]
- **Verified:** 2026-09-11
- **Deadline:** 2026-03-01 (applications opened 2026-01-01)
- **Target Sector:** Any
- **Max Amount:** US$5,000 (non-refundable seed capital)
- **Requires Registration:** No (but you must apply through TEFConnect)
- **Why it fits:** Women make up 46% of TEF entrepreneurs — the highest female
  participation of any programme at this scale. The non-refundable structure makes
  it ideal for informal traders who fear debt.
- **Notes:** Applications are strictly through the TEFConnect portal.

## Grant 6: AFAWA (African Development Bank)
- **Provider:** African Development Bank
- **URL:** https://afawa.afdb.org
- **Verified:** 2026-09-11
- **Deadline:** Rolling
- **Target Sector:** Any
- **Max Amount:** Programmatic — AFAWA has approved $2.5B in investments for women-led businesses
- **Requires Registration:** Yes (works through financial institutions)
- **Why it fits:** The continent's flagship initiative for the $49B gender financing gap.
  Doesn't fund individuals directly — it backs banks that then lend to women. This is a
  strategic partner grant for the pitch, not a direct application. Use it to show market
  validation.
- **Notes:** Reference this in the pitch deck as ecosystem validation.
